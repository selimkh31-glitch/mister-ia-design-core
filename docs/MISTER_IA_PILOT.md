# Pilot — Mister IA 64

Use Mister IA 64 as the first integration target.

## Suggested DNA

- Positioning: local AI/web implementation partner for small businesses and independent professionals.
- Personality: capable, human, precise, contemporary, non-corporate.
- Avoid: sci-fi AI clichés, neon gradients, glass dashboards, anonymous SaaS language.
- Visual archetype: modern local digital studio with editorial confidence.
- Photography: real local business context, people, workspaces and finished client work when available.
- Density: medium-low marketing surfaces; denser case-study details.
- Motion: restrained, quick, tactile.

## First migration target

1. homepage shell;
2. navigation and CTA system;
3. typography and spacing tokens;
4. service blocks;
5. case studies;
6. local SEO pages without cloning the homepage composition.

Do not redesign every page before the homepage direction is validated.
