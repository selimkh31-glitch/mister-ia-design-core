# Local Business Project DNA

## Default strategic bias
- Human trust over technological spectacle.
- Local proof, real photography and clear service geography.
- Strong contact intent without aggressive SaaS conversion language.
- Mobile-first for calls, directions and quote requests.

## Visual prompts
Choose a visual archetype appropriate to the actual trade: editorial, craft, outdoor, hospitality, clinical, automotive, etc. Never use "local business" itself as a visual style.

## Required proof surfaces
- real projects / work;
- service area;
- credentials/reviews where verified;
- visible human identity where appropriate;
- concrete process and contact path.
