## Rules
- Motion explains hierarchy, state or causality.
- Entrance animation should not delay comprehension.
- Hover motion must not move critical hit targets unpredictably.
- Avoid animating layout dimensions when transform/opacity is sufficient.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Timing
- Micro interaction: `--motion-fast`
- Standard transition: `--motion-normal`
- Hero/editorial transition: `--motion-slow` only when justified
